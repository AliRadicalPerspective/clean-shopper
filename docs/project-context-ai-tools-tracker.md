## 1. Problem Statement
The design team has no shared visibility into which AI tools people are using, what they're using them for, or whether the outputs make it into production. As a result, nobody knows what others are experimenting with, and the team keeps buying overlapping subscriptions.

## 2. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Members of an internal design team who are individually experimenting with AI tools, and whoever manages the team's tooling and subscriptions. They are already using AI tools in their day-to-day work but do so in isolation, without knowing what colleagues are trying. Their relationship to the problem is both as contributors of usage information and as consumers of the shared visibility the tool would provide.

## 3. Pain Points
- No one knows what AI tools anyone else on the team is experimenting with.
- Overlapping subscriptions keep getting purchased, wasting spend.
- There is no visibility into what the AI tools are being used for.
- There is no way to tell whether AI-generated outputs are actually making it into production.

## 4. Proposed Solution
An internal tool that lets the design team:
- Track which AI tools each person is using.
- Record what each tool is being used for.
- Track whether the outputs from those tools are making it into production.
